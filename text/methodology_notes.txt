Methodology notes — jot down ideas in your own words
=====================================================

1. What game are agents playing?
   (actions, resources, costs, what makes it a dilemma)


2. What are the three things you're varying and why?
   (IVs, levels, what each tests)


3. How does a single round work, mechanically?
   (observation → decision → resolution → memory update)


4. How does the network work?
   (initial structure, visibility, action restriction, rewiring)


5. How do you measure what happens?
   (DVs, metrics, what counts as "social order")
