\textit{``Whatsoever therefore is consequent to a time of war, where every man is enemy to every man [...] In such condition there is no place for industry, because the fruit thereof is uncertain: and consequently no culture of the earth; no navigation [...] no society; and which is worst of all, continual fear, and danger of violent death; and the life of man, solitary, poor, nasty, brutish, and short."}

\begin{flushright}
Thomas Hobbes, \textit{Leviathan} (1651)
\end{flushright}
