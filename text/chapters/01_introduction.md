I. OPENING - The Enduring Puzzle (1 paragraaf)

Post-Cold War: realism failed to predict peaceful transition, constructivism offered alternative explanation
Core tension remains unresolved: are state behaviors driven by material structure or social construction?
Deze vraag is niet alleen theoretisch - heeft implicaties voor hoe we internationale orde begrijpen en vormgeven

II. THE THEORETICAL DIVIDE (2-3 paragrafen)
A. Waltzian Neorealism

Structure determines outcomes: anarchic system forces states into self-help
Material capabilities define power distribution
States as rational utility maximizers focused on survival/power
Identity and interests are exogenous - states ARE competitive

B. Wendtian Constructivism

Process determines outcomes: "anarchy is what states make of it"
Shared ideas and intersubjective meanings shape behavior
Identity and interests emerge through interaction
States BECOME competitive (or cooperative) through social construction

C. Current State of Debate

Constructivism heeft terrein gewonnen (TRIP data: top cited works)
Maar debat blijft: onder welke condities geldt welke theorie?
Empirische tests blijven nodig

III. THE COMPUTATIONAL TURN (2 paragrafen)
A. Why Computational Methods

ABM enables testing macro-level outcomes from micro-level assumptions
Isolates causal mechanisms impossible in observational data
Growing tradition: Cederman, Axelrod, Epstein & Axtell

B. A Novel Mapping

RL agents = operationalization of realism: pure utility optimizers, material reward maximization
LLM agents = operationalization of constructivism: semantic reasoning over shared history, theory of mind
Same information, different reasoning architectures
Test: do different cognitive mechanisms produce different international orders?

IV. RESEARCH QUESTION & CONTRIBUTION (1-2 paragrafen)
Primary Question:
"Is hierarchy a functional inevitability of optimization, or a cultural artifact of semantic reasoning?"
Sub-questions:

Do pure optimizers (RL) converge on hierarchical, self-help systems?
Do semantic reasoners (LLM) enable cooperative, norm-based orders?
What scope conditions determine which outcome emerges?

Contribution:

First computational test mapping agent architectures to IR paradigms
Isolates cognitive mechanisms underlying theoretical predictions
Extends ABM in IR beyond rule-based agents to learning systems
