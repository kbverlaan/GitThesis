# 3. Research Question(s)

## Primary Research Question

**Does complex political order emerge from material constraints alone, regardless of semantic understanding?**
I think we cannot frame the LLMs as "semantic understanding". Thats to big.

## Sub-Questions

### Q1: Do pure optimizers reach complex political order?

### Q2: Does semantic awareness improve coordination outcomes?
Does semantic awareness of social concepts ("loyalty," "betrayal," "alliance") improve coordination outcomes compared to optimization alone?

*Operationalization*: Do LLM agents (semantic prompt) show higher bond stability, lower conflict rates, or different structural patterns than RL agents?

### Q3: Does changing the parameters of the environment result in similar dynamics?

## Hypotheses

**H2 (Semantic Priors Matter)**: LLM (semantic) shows higher bond stability and lower conflict than RL
- *Reasoning*: Semantic priors ("loyalty is good") act as equilibrium selection mechanism

**H3 (Physics Dominates Culture)**: At high conflict cost (c > 5), all agent types converge
- *Reasoning*: When war is expensive enough, material constraints force cooperation

## Null Hypothesis

There is no systematic difference in structural outcomes (Gini, stability, topology) or behavioral patterns (investment rate, conflict rate) across agent architectures under identical environmental parameters.
